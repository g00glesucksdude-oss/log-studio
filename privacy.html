ADPCM Log Studio: Privacy Policy

ADPCM Log Studio is a personal tool. It is used only by its owner, to merge audio recordings and publish the result.

What it accesses. With the owner's permission it uses the owner's Google Drive to store uploaded recordings and results, and the owner's YouTube channel to upload videos.

What it stores. Nothing is collected by or sent to any third-party server. Files stay in the owner's own Google Drive, and processing runs in the owner's own GitHub Actions account. Sign-in details are kept as encrypted secrets in the owner's GitHub repository or in the owner's browser.

Sharing. Data is not sold, shared or used for advertising. Finished files are published only where the owner chooses: a YouTube video set to unlisted, and a public Drive link.

Deleting data. The owner can delete files from Drive and YouTube at any time, and can revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
